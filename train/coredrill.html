How to read this

A territory's center is the average of its texts; its radius (sigma) is how far they spread from it. Two territories answer well when their centers are apart: below a distance of 0.1 a text between them is close to a coin toss, and at 0 they are one territory under two names. The taxonomy decides where things stand; the move of a ball is the change in the model's own distances inside its domain, and the thin trail shows where it came from. The whole space may turn between two trainings; that turn is taken out before anything is compared.
